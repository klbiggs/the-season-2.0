# P21 — Ledger Completeness & Notifications (v1.1 hardening)

> **Status: 📋 planned.** Track: v1.1 (UI-correctness, no new gameplay rules). Source: [MASTER.md §11.1](../MASTER.md#111-ui-surfacing-gaps-v1-fix-candidates) + [§11.3](../MASTER.md#113-plan--doc-inconsistencies).
> Gives every persisted subsystem a readout and bridges tick-later async outcomes into the core loop. Closes the remaining §11.1 gaps after P20.

## Depends on
P14 (ledger shell + tabs + `useShallow` selector convention + `ActionRejectionToast`), P20 (core-loop surfaces, `ProposalOutcomeBanner`, the App-chrome notification-banner precedent, the BallroomView faux-pas comment fix).

## Goal
Every persisted `GameState` subsystem **still** flagged "Not-surfaced / Partial" after P20 (duels, marriages, couriers, happiness/composure/temptation, proposals/tutor logs — NOT generation/ancestorSummary, which P20 already closed in `TopBar.tsx`) has a ledger readout, and tick-later scheme outcomes reach the player without a ledger dig. Plus the four §11.3 doc inconsistencies are reconciled in code/plan.

## Decisions (resolved this session)

Resolved against the shipped code; the rest of the plan assumes them.

1. **Duels and marriages are SUB-SECTIONS in existing tabs, not new tabs.** Duels fold into `GrievancesTab` (a duel is the resolution of a grievance — `Duel.grievanceId` FK at `entities.ts:441`); marriages fold into `FamilyTreeTab`. This holds the tab strip at **10** and matches the pervasive P14 fold-into-existing-tab convention (sibling-rivals under Family Tree, inherited-secrets/blood-feuds under Rivals, faction-tension under Patronesses). The skeleton's "new section or tab" fork is resolved to **section**.
2. **Couriers readout lives in `StaffTab`**, beside the `SpymasterInterceptCard` it makes legible (the card acts on `state.couriers` blind today). Not WhispersTab — the action that consumes couriers (Spymaster intercept + InterceptMail staff) is the Staff surface.
3. **Scheme-outcome notification covers player-launched schemes AND rival schemes that complete against a player-house target** (user decision this session). Relevance predicate per event: `event.ownerHouseId === PLAYER_HOUSE_ID` **OR** (the event carries a non-null `targetCharacterId` whose `store.getState().characters.get(targetCharacterId)?.houseId === PLAYER_HOUSE_ID` — **null-guard the deref**: `SchemeCompleted.targetCharacterId` is `CharacterId | null`, `scheme.ts:56`, and a genuinely-absent id is possible). `SchemeFailed` carries **no** `targetCharacterId` (only `reason`, `scheme.ts:58-65`), so it is player-owned-only by nature — a rival's sabotage *failing* against the player is correctly not notified. The "rival's scheme succeeded against you" surface is the new bit beyond §11.1's "launched-scheme" wording; it reads only already-published `schemeBus` events, so it adds no new design state.
4. **Notification surface = App-chrome dismissible banner**, mirroring P20's `elopement-outcome-banner` (`App.tsx:81-111`) rather than the auto-dismiss `ActionRejectionToast`. Scheme outcomes can resolve during a Map `advanceTick` or a year-pass; the player may be on any view, and a self-dismissing 3s toast risks being missed for a +25-scandal `SeductionDiscovered`. Single most-recent banner (last-write-wins within a tick; the `SchemesTab` history is the complete record). Manually dismissed; no auto-timeout. **In-ballroom scheme-launch rejections** (LOW item) DO use the auto-dismiss `ActionRejectionToast` — those are transient race feedback, not tick-later payoffs.

## In scope

### HIGH — buried payoffs get a readout

- **Duels history sub-section in `GrievancesTab`.** New "Duels" sub-section (`data-testid="grievances-duels-list"`) below the existing Resolved section, rendering `state.duels` (`Map<DuelId, Duel>`, `entities.ts:616`) lex-sorted by id. Per-row fields (all live on `DuelSchema`, `entities.ts:437-448`): challenger name + defender name (resolved via `state.characters.get`), `status` (Active/Resolved), `outcome` (`ChallengerVictory`/`DefenderVictory`/`Stalemate`, nullable until resolved), `loserCasualty` (`Killed`/`Wounded`/`Unharmed`), `initiatedAtTick`, `resolvedAtTick` (nullable), `exchangesElapsed` (0-12). Cross-reference the triggering grievance via `duel.grievanceId`. Read-only — duels are created/resolved synchronously by `demandSatisfaction` (the existing action in the same tab); the just-resolved outcome still flashes from the React-local `result` state (`GrievancesTab.tsx:33,57`), but now the persisted history persists across re-renders. **Player-relevance filter:** show duels where the challenger OR defender is a player-house character (resolve house via `characters.get(...).houseId`); v1 only ever mints player-involved duels (`demandSatisfaction` is the sole duel source), so the filter is defensive but cheap.
- **Marriages roster sub-section in `FamilyTreeTab`.** New "Marriages" sub-section (`data-testid="family-marriages-list"`) rendering `state.marriages` (`Map<MarriageId, MarriageRecord>`, `entities.ts:569`) lex-sorted by id, filtered to marriages where `spouseAId` or `spouseBId` resolves to a player-house character. Per-row (fields live on `MarriageRecordSchema`, `entities.ts:301-314`): both spouse names (resolved via `characters.get`; show `"— (deceased/unknown)"` fallback), `marriedAtTick`, a `dissolved` badge with `dissolvedAtTick`, and a `viaElopement` badge when true. Active marriages render plainly; dissolved ones render dimmed. This compounds with P20's now-reachable `proposeMarriage` — the player can finally see the marriages they make.

### MEDIUM — partial subsystems completed

- **Couriers readout in `StaffTab`.** New "Couriers" sub-section (`data-testid="staff-couriers-list"`) below the Spymaster Action block, rendering `state.couriers` (`Map<CourierPayloadId, CourierPayload>`, `entities.ts:594`). Per-row (fields on `CourierPayloadSchema`, `entities.ts:368-382`): `status` (`InFlight`/`Delivered`/`Intercepted`/`Cancelled` per `COURIER_STATUSES`), `direction` (Inbound/Outbound), origin→destination house (resolved via `houseDisplayName`), `originTick` → absolute `arrivalTick`, ticks-remaining derived as `Math.max(0, arrivalTick - state.tick)` for in-flight rows, an `interceptable` flag badge, and the linked whisper id (`whisperId`). Split into "In Flight" (status `InFlight`, sorted by `arrivalTick` ascending) and a collapsed "Resolved" history (everything else, sorted by `resolvedAtTick` descending). This makes the `SpymasterInterceptCard`'s "drop the lex-smallest inbound interceptable courier" affordance legible — the player can see what exists before spending the wealth.
- **Happiness / composure / temptation-latch in `FamilyTreeTab` `CharacterRow`.** Extend the existing per-character row (`FamilyTreeTab.tsx:25-58`) with three reads already on `CharacterSchema`: `happiness` (0-100, `entities.ts:233`), `composure` (clamped `[0, maxComposure]`, `entities.ts:228-229` — show `composure/maxComposure`), and a "tempted" pill when `temptationHookUnlocked === true` (`entities.ts:236`). Add a small "Dull-spouse exposure since t{N}" affordance when `dullSpouseExposureStartTick !== null` (`entities.ts:245`) so the Dull-spouse → happiness-drain → temptation-unlock flow is visible end-to-end. Keep the row scannable — happiness/composure as compact numeric cells; temptation/exposure as conditional pills (mirrors the existing `equipped` pill pattern).
- **Scheme-outcome notification banner on App chrome.** Subscribe `App.tsx` to `schemeBus` via `useBusEvent` for `SchemeCompleted`, `SchemeFailed`, and `SeductionDiscovered` (the three §11.1-named events; `scheme.ts:50-72`). On a player-relevant event (Decision 3 predicate), set a `schemeNotice` state and render a dismissible banner styled like the elopement banner (`data-testid="scheme-outcome-banner"`). Prose maps per event:
  - `SchemeCompleted` (owner = player): "Your {schemeKind} {against {targetName} }succeeded." `SchemeCompleted` (rival, target = player house): "A rival's {schemeKind} against {targetName} succeeded."
  - `SchemeFailed` (owner = player): "Your {schemeKind} failed — {reason prose}." (reason from the `SchemeFailureReasonPayload` discriminant: `TargetDiedDuringFlight` → "the target died mid-scheme"; `EraFlippedDuringSmuggling` → "the era turned before the run completed").
  - `SeductionDiscovered` (owner = player): "Your Seduction of {targetName} was discovered." Target name resolved via `store.getState().characters.get(...)`. Single most-recent notice; manual dismiss.

## Implementation notes
- **Bus subscription cleanup.** App-chrome `schemeBus` subscription uses the `useBusEvent` hook (`App.tsx` already imports it for `elopementBus`) — it owns its own `useEffect` cleanup, satisfying the §9 "every subscription pairs with a cleanup" non-negotiable. Three `useBusEvent` calls (one per event kind) or one shared handler factory; keep them top-level in the component (hooks rules).
- **Banner vs toast split is deliberate** (Decision 4): tick-later scheme *outcomes* → persistent App-chrome banner (player may be on any view, may have just clicked advance-tick from the Map); transient *rejection* feedback (scheme-launch race, enter-ballroom race) → 3s `ActionRejectionToast`. Don't conflate.
- **Sub-section, not tab.** Every new readout is a `<div>`/`<section>` appended inside an existing tab component — no `LedgerView.tsx` `TabKey` union change, no new `TABS` entry. The tab strip stays at 10 and the `ledger-tabbtn-*` / `ledger-tab-*` testids are untouched.
- **`useShallow` selector discipline.** New reads in `GrievancesTab` (add `duels`), `FamilyTreeTab` (already pulls `characters`/`houses`; add `marriages`, `proposalsLog`, `tutorAssignmentsLog`), and `StaffTab` (currently selector-free — add a `useShallow` bundle for `couriers` + `houses` + `tick`) all go through `useShallow` per the P14 "single re-render per tick" pin. `StaffTab` becomes a reading component (it was a pure wrapper); keep the wrapped panels unchanged.
- **Name-resolution helper.** Duels/marriages/couriers all resolve `CharacterId`/`HouseId` → display name. Reuse `houseDisplayName` (`src/ui/ledger/houseName.ts`, already used by `GrievancesTab`) for houses; `state.characters.get(id)?.name ?? "—"` for characters (deceased characters persist — `alive: false`, never deleted — so most lookups resolve; the `?? "—"` guards genuinely-absent ids).
- **Nullable-field guards.** `Duel.outcome` / `resolvedAtTick` are null until resolved; `MarriageRecord.dissolvedAtTick` is null unless dissolved; `CourierPayload.resolvedAtTick` is null while in-flight. Render `"—"` (em-dash) for null cells, never the literal `null` (the P14 `RivalsTab` precedent).
- **`SchemeFailed` has no target.** Don't index `event.targetCharacterId` on `SchemeFailed` — it's absent from that variant (`scheme.ts:58-65`). TS enforces this if the handler switches on `event.kind` before reading kind-specific fields.
- **No deterministic seam for the scheme banner in Playwright** beyond driving real schemes; prefer a Vitest/component test that publishes `SchemeCompleted`/`SchemeFailed`/`SeductionDiscovered` directly on `schemeBus` and asserts the App-chrome banner renders with the right prose + relevance filtering (the P20 faux-pas-verification precedent: publish on the bus, assert the subscriber renders). The relevance predicate (player-owned vs rival-against-player vs irrelevant) is the key thing to unit-test.
