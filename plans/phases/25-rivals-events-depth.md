### R2. Rival economy — wealth becomes real
- Delete the `RIVAL_HOUSE_IDS_SET` skip in `applyEconomyPhase` (`src/game/rules/economy.ts:41`) — this phase is **bankruptcy-tier decay, NOT location income**.
